What is MLSB?

A whole web presence, without building a website

MLSB is a hosted platform with three simple parts working together — so customers can find you and you stay in control.

Your mini-site

A single, mobile-friendly page at your own short URL — description, contact details, gallery, price list or menu, and a contact form. Pick a template and colour scheme that fits.

The public directory

A searchable homepage where customers look by keyword, category and area. Your business shows up wherever you operate — a single town, a county, a region, or UK-wide.

Your dashboard

An owner control centre to edit content, reorder sections, switch templates and watch weekly and monthly visitor counts — all without touching code.
