Clubs, charities & not-for-profits

A membership to look after, not just customers to reach

Charities, not-for-profits, societies, clubs and associations get everything above — and a complete membership system in the same account. Who belongs, the meetings you hold, the emails you send them and the subs they pay, in one place instead of three spreadsheets.

It appears in your dashboard automatically when you register as a charity, not-for-profit or social group, and it's part of the free plan.

Your register
✓

Membership register

Everyone who belongs, with their own membership number, contact details, the date they joined and every role they hold — chairperson, secretary, treasurer and the rest. Members who leave move to a former members list with the date they went, so you can always answer who was a member and when.

✓

Your own tags & notes

Label members the way you already think of them — Committee, Life member, Subs unpaid — and filter the register by them, with notes against anyone who needs them. Both stay in your admin and never appear on your public page.

✓

Bring your list with you

Import your members from a spreadsheet or a Google Contacts export — column headings are matched for you, and you see exactly what will happen before anything is saved. Export the whole register to CSV or JSON whenever you like.

Meetings & messages
✓

Meetings & attendance

Record each meeting with its venue and speaker. Every current member starts out invited, so you take people off rather than adding everyone on, and marking the register is one click each: accepted, declined, attended or did not show.

✓

Emails to your members

Write from templates you own, with ready-made ones — meeting reminder, change of venue, welcome a new member — to start from. Each email fills in the member's preferred name, their membership number and the meeting's date, venue and speaker, and can go to everyone or just the people on one meeting.

✓

Replies without the chasing

Meeting emails carry Yes / No / Not sure buttons. Each member gets a private link, answers with one tap, and the attendance list updates itself — nothing to type back in.

Subs, venues & contacts
✓

Subscriptions & payments

Set up annual or monthly subs, meeting charges and event fees, with every current member added at the usual amount. Change a figure for a concession or a waiver, mark who has paid, and see what is still owing and what is overdue across the whole organisation.

✓

The halls you meet at

Keep your venues with the caretaker or hall secretary you actually ring for the key. It is the same record your events and bookings use, so an address corrected once is corrected everywhere it appears.

✓

External contacts

A contacts book for everyone who isn't a member — suppliers, guest speakers, the printer who does the newsletter, the insurer — searchable and taggable, and imported the same way as the register.

Also included: settings for how often you meet and where, so a new meeting comes part-filled; and the option to include members in your newsletter once they have confirmed they are happy to hear from you.

FAQ

Common questions

What exactly do I get?

A one-page website at mlsb.uk/yourname with your description, contact details, opening hours, photos, prices and a contact form — plus a dashboard to manage it all. Extras such as events, online bookings and newsletters can be switched on if you want them.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes — £0, forever. Every verified business gets a live website, and no card details are needed unless you choose to upgrade.

Do I need my own domain name?

No. Your website lives at mlsb.uk/yourname, so there's nothing to buy or renew. If you already have a domain, you can display it on your website.

Do I need any technical skills?

None. Everything is managed from a simple dashboard — nothing to install, host or update.

How quickly will my website be live?

As soon as your account is verified. When you register we email you a confirmation link, and we ask you to confirm that you are the owner of the business. Once that's done your website goes live at mlsb.uk/yourname, and you can add and refine your content whenever suits you.

Do I have to use the bookings system?

Not at all. Bookings are entirely optional and switched off by default. Plenty of businesses just want a smart page with their prices, photos and a contact form — that works perfectly well on its own.

How do bookings and the diary work?

If you turn them on, you add bookable appointments, rooms or venues and set your availability. Customers choose a time online, then you both receive confirmation and cancellation emails.

We're a club or a charity rather than a business — can we use MLSB?

Yes. Register under Charities & not-for-profits or Associations & social groups and your dashboard gains a Membership Management area: a register of your members with their membership numbers and roles, club meetings and attendance, emails to members with Yes / No RSVP buttons, subscriptions and payments, the halls you meet at and a contacts book. It's included on the free plan.

How much does Premium cost, and can I cancel?

£5 a month or £50 a year (two months free). You can cancel at any time and drop back to the free plan; after the initial grace period, payments aren't refunded.

What happens when my free trial ends?

Your account reverts to Free automatically and nothing is charged. Free-tier limits then apply to any extra entries, but nothing is deleted — upgrade later and everything comes back.
