## What you get — the full list

**Free features (included, always):**

- Business details — logo, addresses, phone numbers and your main website
- Opening hours — set the days and times you're open
- Interactive Google map — shows your location and gives visitors directions
- Categories and tags — help people find you
- Operating areas — the towns, counties and regions you cover
- Design templates — choose your layout, colours and styling
- Image galleries — upload photos for visitors to browse
- Call-out boxes — a heading, text and an optional picture and link
- Team and staff — list the key people in your business
- Contact form — let visitors send you a message
- Price lists — show what you charge
- Food menus — for cafés, restaurants and anyone serving food and drink
- Articles — publish news, a blog, or tips and advice
- Social links — connect your social media
- Policy pages — ready-made privacy and terms pages

**Paid features (available now, or by the end of July):**

- Multiple opening-hours sets — for seasonal or changing hours
- More design templates — with new ones added regularly
- Extra gallery styles — fading, sliding and other effects
- Multiple contact forms — build your own and route messages to specific staff
- Custom policy pages — create and edit your own

**Coming soon (paid):**

- Booking system — styled for different kinds of business
- Newsletter management — create and send newsletters to your own subscribers
- Event management — advertise events you're attending or running

**Coming later:**

- Ticket management — sell or send out tickets for your events
- E-invoices — invoice customers and take payment through the booking system
- E-commerce — sell products online, including refunds and complaints

The 30-day trial is available whenever you'd like to explore the paid features.
